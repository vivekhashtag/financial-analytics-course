## Rules of the road

1. **Notebooks are where learning happens.** Reading is 30%; typing is 70%.
2. Solution notebooks unlock only after you submit an attempt.
3. Prerequisites are suggested, never enforced, but skipping Module 2 will hurt in Module 3.
4. The **four biases** ritual (Module 1) repeats in every lab. By Module 12 it should be reflex.
