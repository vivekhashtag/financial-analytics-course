## Why they're synthetic

All five are generated by a script with a fixed random seed. That's deliberate, for three reasons:

1. **Licensing.** Real market data can't be redistributed freely.
2. **Teaching.** We can plant *exactly* the defects you need to find, and we document every one.
3. **Auditability.** Anyone can regenerate byte-identical data. That's Module 1's sixth quality pillar, applied to our own course.

<Callout type="info">
You will also pull **real** NSE data live via `yfinance` in Modules 3, 6 and 9. Synthetic data teaches the skill; real data proves you have it.
</Callout>

## The dataset registry

Every dataset is documented, source, grain, units, and known quirks. Look at `messy_transactions`: we tell you upfront there are 54 duplicates, 12 unmarked USD amounts, and 9 rows dated 1970-01-01.

Knowing the answer key doesn't spoil the exercise. Finding those defects *with code*, and knowing what to do about each one, is the exercise.
