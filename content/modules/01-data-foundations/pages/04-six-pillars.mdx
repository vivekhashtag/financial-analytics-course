## The student translation

Think about your own marksheet:

- **Accuracy**, the marks are right
- **Completeness**, no subject is missing
- **Consistency**, your name is spelled the same on every certificate
- **Timeliness**, results arrive before the admission deadline
- **Lineage**, the question paper and your answer sheet still exist
- **Auditability**, re-totalling gives the same result

Same six questions. Bigger numbers.
