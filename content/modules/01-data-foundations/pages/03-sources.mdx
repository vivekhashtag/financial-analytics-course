## The golden source

Professionals never ask "what's the price?" They ask: **"what's the price, according to which source, as of when?"**

The same stock shows different closing prices across free sources, different exchanges, different adjustment conventions, different time zones. This is why reconciliation exists as a job.

<PriceDiscrepancy exerciseId="m1-e-price" />

Three sources report yesterday's close for the same stock as ₹102.50, ₹102.50 and ₹101.87. The third isn't wrong, it's **unadjusted for a dividend**. Adjusted vs unadjusted prices is a trap that returns with real consequences in Module 3, and it's sitting inside one of your own datasets right now.
