Notebook 3D produces the four charts every analyst makes on day one with any asset:

1. **Price + moving averages**, `rolling(50).mean()`; the crossovers feed Lab 3's strategy
2. **Return distribution vs the bell curve**, *the most important chart in quantitative finance*: real markets have fat tails, and models assuming normality understate risk exactly when it matters
3. **Drawdown curve**, `close / close.cummax() − 1`: the pain chart on every fund factsheet
4. **Rolling volatility**, where Module 1's "regime change" stops being a warning and becomes a visible step in the chart

Two conventions to memorise: **×252** annualises a mean daily return; **×√252** annualises daily volatility (risks add in variance, not standard deviation: Module 10 will make you feel why).
